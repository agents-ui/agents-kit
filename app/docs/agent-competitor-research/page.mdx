# Agent Competitor Research

Present structured competitive analysis findings from AI research agents with side-by-side comparisons and threat assessments.

## Features

- **Competitor Cards**: Individual profile cards for each competitor with key stats and positioning
- **Comparison Matrix**: Side-by-side feature and capability comparison across competitors
- **Threat Assessment**: Color-coded threat levels and strategic risk indicators
- **Key Findings**: AI-generated summary of the most critical competitive insights
- **Research Depth**: Confidence scores and source counts reflecting analysis thoroughness
